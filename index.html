Project Overview

AI Microscope introduces an Offline AI-Assisted Workstation designed for the Microbiology Laboratory at Mbeya Regional Referral Hospital (MRRH).

Clinical Impact

  • Faster diagnosis enabling targeted antibiotic therapy
  • Reduced antibiotic resistance through precision medicine
  • Improved patient outcomes
  • Support for low-resource clinical environments
×

Technical Specifications

  • Base Architecture: EfficientNetV2M
  • Framework: TensorFlow 2.18.1 + Keras 3.x
  • Classes: 34 bacterial species
  • Input: 224x224 RGB images
  • Parameters: 105.7M
×

Performance Metrics

  • Processing: Under 5 seconds per sample
  • Confidence Threshold: 90% minimum
  • Dataset: DiBaS (Digital Bacterial System)
  • Operation: Completely offline
