# Frequently Asked Questions

---

## Getting Started

### How do I start the AI Microscope?

**Double-click** the AI Microscope icon on your desktop. The application will open automatically.

### Do I need special training?

No! The AI Microscope is designed for laboratory technicians. Just follow the on-screen instructions.

### What do I need to use the system?

- A computer with Windows 10 or 11
- A microscope camera (USB connection)
- Your microscope slides with bacterial samples

---

## Camera Issues

### My camera isn't working. What should I do?

1. **Check the connection** - Make sure the USB cable is plugged in
2. **Try a different USB port** - Some ports work better than others
3. **Restart the application** - Close and reopen the program
4. **Restart the computer** - If nothing else works, restart your computer

### The image is blurry. How can I fix it?

1. **Adjust the microscope focus** - Turn the focus knob on your microscope
2. **Clean the lens** - Use lens cleaning solution and a soft cloth
3. **Check lighting** - Ensure adequate light on your sample
4. **Use image adjustments** - In Settings, adjust brightness and contrast

### Can I use my phone camera instead?

No, the system requires a microscope camera connected to the computer. Phone cameras cannot be used directly.

## Diagnosis Questions

### How accurate is the AI diagnosis?

The AI is **94.2% accurate** based on clinical testing with 500 samples. Always verify results if the confidence score is below 90%.

### What does the confidence score mean?

The confidence score shows how sure the AI is about its result:

- **90% or higher**: Very reliable - trust this result
- **70-89%**: Reliable - consider confirming with additional tests
- **Below 70%**: Not reliable - must confirm with other methods

### How long does a diagnosis take?

Typically **5-10 seconds**. If it takes longer, check your internet connection and close other applications.

### What is the heatmap for?

The heatmap shows **where the AI is looking** in the image:

- **Red areas**: Most important for the diagnosis
- **Yellow areas**: Moderately important
- **Blue areas**: Less important

This helps you verify the AI is focusing on the right parts of the image.

### Can I trust the results?

For **clinical decisions**, only trust results with 90%+ confidence. Always use your professional judgment and confirm uncertain results with standard laboratory tests.

## Records and Data

### Where are my records saved?

Records are automatically saved in the application's database. You can view them in the **Records** tab.

### Can I delete old records?

Yes. Select the record and click the **🗑️ Delete** button. **Warning:** Deleted records cannot be recovered.

### How do I export my data?

1. Go to the **Records** tab
2. Select the records you want to export
3. Click **📤 Export**
4. Choose PDF or CSV format
5. Save to your desired location

### Can I print reports?

Yes. Export as PDF, then open and print the PDF file.

## Settings and Configuration

### How do I change the camera?

Go to **Settings → Camera Settings** and select a different camera from the dropdown menu.

### What is the best resolution to use?

For clinical use, **1280x720** or **1920x1080** is recommended. Higher resolutions take more space but provide more detail.

### Should I enable auto-enhance?

Yes, for most users. It automatically adjusts brightness and contrast for better images.

### What confidence threshold should I use?

For clinical work, set it to **0.90 (90%)**. This ensures only highly confident results are used for diagnosis.

## Clinical Use

### Can I use this for patient diagnosis?

Yes, but only for results with **90%+ confidence**. Always confirm uncertain results with standard laboratory methods.

### Do I need to enter Patient ID?

This depends on your lab's settings. If enabled, you must enter a Patient ID before saving results.

### Is my data secure?

Yes. All patient data is encrypted and stored locally. Only authorized personnel can access it.

### Can I use this without internet?

Yes, but the first launch requires internet to load the AI model. After that, it works offline.

## Performance

### The application is slow. What can I do?

1. Close other applications
2. Restart your computer
3. Check available disk space (need at least 10GB free)
4. Ensure your computer meets the minimum requirements

### My computer gets hot when using the app. Is this normal?

Yes, AI processing requires significant computing power. Ensure:
- Good ventilation
- No blocking of air vents
- Clean dust from fans regularly

## Updates and Maintenance

### How do I update the software?

The system checks for updates automatically. If an update is available, you'll see a notification. Click to install.

### How often should I calibrate the camera?

**Monthly** calibration is recommended for best results. Contact IT for calibration procedures.

### Should I backup my data?

Yes. Export your records regularly and save them to a secure location or network drive.
