# Getting Started with AI Microscope

## Welcome to AI Microscope 🏥

The AI Microscope helps you identify bacterial species quickly and accurately using artificial intelligence.

---

## Quick Start

### Step 1: Launch the Application

**Double-click** the AI Microscope icon on your desktop to start the application.

### Step 2: Prepare Your Sample

1. **Prepare your microscope slide** with the bacterial sample
2. **Place the slide** under the microscope
3. **Adjust the focus** until the image is clear

### Step 3: Capture the Image

1. Click the **📷 Capture Image** button
2. The camera will take a picture of your sample
3. Review the image to ensure it's clear

### Step 4: Run AI Diagnosis

1. Click the **🔬 Run Diagnosis** button
2. Wait for the AI to analyze the image (usually 5-10 seconds)
3. View the results on your screen

### Step 5: Save the Results

1. Enter the **Patient ID** (if required)
2. Click **💾 Save Record** to store the results
3. The system automatically saves the image and diagnosis

---

## Understanding Your Results

### Confidence Score

The AI shows how confident it is in its diagnosis:

- **🟢 90% or higher**: Very reliable result
- **🟡 70-89%**: Reliable result, consider confirming
- **🔴 Below 70%**: Confirm with additional tests

### Heatmap Visualization

The colored overlay shows where the AI is looking:

- **Red areas**: Most important for the diagnosis
- **Yellow areas**: Moderately important
- **Blue areas**: Less important

This helps you understand which parts of the image influenced the decision.

## Tips for Best Results

✅ **Use clear, well-focused images**
✅ **Ensure proper lighting**
✅ **Clean the microscope lens regularly**
✅ **Calibrate the camera monthly**

❌ **Avoid blurry or dark images**
❌ **Don't use contaminated slides**
❌ **Never skip quality checks**
